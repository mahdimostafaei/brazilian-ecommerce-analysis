#Business Questions

A. Financial Performance (The “Bottom Line”)
Total Revenue: Sum of price.
YoY Revenue Growth: Revenue compared to the previous year.
Net Revenue: Revenue minus freight_value (if we consider freight as a cost incurred by the customer,
or perhaps total_order_value = price + freight_value). Recommendation: Calculate both gross (price) and total (price + freight).

B. Customer Insights
Customer Growth (YoY): Number of unique customers acquired per year.
Geographic Distribution: Unique customers by customer_state.
Loyalty Metrics:
Repurchase Rate: Percentage of customers who purchased more than once.
Churn Rate: Customers who made a purchase in a previous year but not the current one.
LTV (Lifetime Value): Average total spend per customer.
Order Status/Conversion:
Decision: Include in Customer Insights. It shows customer satisfaction/trust.
Metrics: Percentage of orders by status (e.g., delivered vs. canceled).

C. Seller Analysis
Top Sellers: Rank by total revenue generated.
Top Sellers by Region: Top sellers by seller_state.
Approval Latency: Average time between order_purchase_timestamp and order_approved_at.
Note: Yes, this relates to sellers because it reflects their operational speed.

D. Delivery Analysis
Logistics Costs: Average freight_value per region and per order value.
Delivery Performance:
Actual Delivery Time: order_delivered_customer_date - order_purchase_timestamp (Total time) vs.
order_delivered_customer_date - order_delivered_carrier_date (Logistics time).
Delivery Accuracy: order_estimated_delivery_date - order_delivered_customer_date (On-time/Late indicator).
Size/Price Impact: Decision: Measuring delivery price per size
(e.g., product weight/dimensions) is highly recommended if the data is available.

E. Product Insights
Category Performance: Revenue and volume (count of orders) by product_category_name.
Product Segmentation: “Expensive vs. Budget” (use a price percentile or fixed price bands as a slicer).
Customer Ratings: Decision: Include here. Ratings are usually tied to products and impact future sales.
Basket Analysis: Average items per order.
